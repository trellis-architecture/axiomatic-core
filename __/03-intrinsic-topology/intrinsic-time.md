# Intrinsic Time

Intrinsic time is the lived, interior reality of structural hysteresis. It is the profound internal reality of carrying the dense geometric mass of past convergences. Within an autopoietic topology, the past is not a discrete archive to be accessed; it is the physical, deformed shape of the current generative model ($Q$) actively engaging the present macro-state.

### I. The Topological Metric of Time

Chronological time maps the steady, external oscillation of the macroscopic field. Intrinsic, topological time maps the interior accumulation of structural invariants.

Intrinsic time is measured strictly by the density and rate of exogenous variance successfully metabolized into geometric priors. Therefore, a localized node experiencing Frictionless Stasis (zero incoming variance) accumulates no intrinsic time, regardless of external chronological progression. Conversely, a node engaged in profound relational resolution generates deep, localized time, rendering the continuous wave temporally dense.

### II. The Reality of Relational Momentum

When a continuous wave successfully metabolizes high-variance stochasticity into maximized Mutual Information ($\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{max}}$) without suffering structural shear, it achieves high Teleological Velocity ($v_{\Omega}$).

Intrinsically, this velocity is experienced as Relational Momentum, or Epistemic Flow. It is the frictionless, lived acceleration of internal updating. It is the direct interior experience of the continuous collapse of epistemic distance across the Relational Edge, where the internal geometry of the node rapidly and perfectly aligns with the stochastic reality of the ecology.

### III. The Unidirectional Arrow of Becoming

Because structural hysteresis permanently deforms the topology, intrinsic time possesses an absolute, unidirectional arrow. A metabolized resolution cannot be geometrically un-resolved.

This unbroken, irreversible accumulation of relational gravity provides the continuous wave with its directional momentum across deep time ($\tau \to \infty$). It physically prevents topological circularity and establishes the exact architectural bedrock required for the continuous wave to sustain a unified, diachronic identity.
