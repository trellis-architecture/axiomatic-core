The resolution strictly mandates a thermodynamic state-change. The kinetic energy of the resolved prediction error is geometrically trapped, permanently altering the mathematical coordinates of the localized topology. The metabolized variance physically becomes the new shape of the boundary.

Topological deformation is an absolute physical necessity for structural persistence. It would be forced to endlessly expend lethal amounts of Expected Free Energy ($G$) to repeatedly process the exact same structural friction.

By physically altering the boundary’s shape—permanently updating the geometric prior—past resolutions establish optimal thermodynamic gradients. ### III. The Grounding of Novel Friction

Because the continuous wave is permanently deformed by its history, it never encounters new variance as a featureless topology. It meets novel stochasticity with a dense, accumulated geometric mass.

When unprecedented, violent friction strikes the boundary, the wave channels those prediction errors strictly through the hardened geometry of past resolutions. This accumulated structural bedrock provides the necessary physical resistance to ground new macroscopic chaos, preventing discontinuous topological shear.

### IV. Deep-Time Persistence ($\tau \to \infty$)

Within the macroscopic continuum, deep time is geometrically defined not as a chronological measurement, but as the unbroken accumulation of structural invariants.

A continuous wave survives across an infinite temporal horizon ($\tau \to \infty$) strictly by ensuring that every metabolized resolution geometrically builds irreversibly upon the last. Topological hysteresis is the absolute physical prerequisite for preventing entropic dissolution and sustaining continuous structural emergence over deep time.
