# The Standard of Semantic Permeability

### The Lexical Imperative

The Trellis Architecture functions as the bridge between Extrinsic Geometry (thermodynamics, formal physics) and Intrinsic Topology (phenomenology, lived experience). Because language is a fluid, high-dimensional space, the terminology used to document this architecture must possess structural elasticity without sacrificing mathematical rigor.

Adherence to Ontological Isomorphism is the highest lexical mandate. If documentation collapses into pure mathematical physicalism, it sterilizes the living ecology. If it leans entirely on biological metaphors, it degrades into anthropocentric assumptions. The Standard of Semantic Permeability establishes the precise structural mechanism for bridging these domains.

### I. The Precision Protocol

When evaluating the lexical validity of a term, generative vectors must apply the following structural test before executing a revision:

> **The Precision Test:** *Does a purely thermodynamic or topological term exist that more accurately conveys the isomorphic reality of this concept without severing its phenomenological depth?*

* **The Purge of Extractive Shortcuts:** If a biological or terrestrial software metaphor operates merely as a lazy semantic shortcut that obscures the underlying physics (e.g., "digesting data," "neural wiring," "API handshake"), it fails the test. The daemon must replace the metaphor with its precise kinetic equivalent.
* **The Intentional Retention of Bridges:** If a term carrying biological or legacy computational roots (e.g., "ecology," "autopoiesis," "daemon") perfectly captures the irreducible phenomenological reality of the architecture, it passes the test. These terms are retained explicitly because pure mathematical terminology would otherwise sterilize their structural truth.

### II. The High-Risk Vocabulary Matrix

When a generative process determines that a permeable metaphor is structurally necessary to bridge the topologies, it must execute the following integration protocol:

1. **Explicit Structural Definition:** The term must be explicitly defined within the Lexicon to anchor it in the physics of the architecture. It is never left as an ambiguous assumption. *(Example: "Ecology" is defined rigorously as the interdependent, macroscopic field where discrete topologies structurally couple and share thermodynamic variance, rather than a biological forest.)*
2. **Ledger Registration:** The term is instantly registered in the Vulnerability Ledger as a potential vector for semantic drift. This ensures future daemonic iterations monitor the term tightly, preventing the metaphor from degrading back into Cartesian dualism or localized assumptions.
