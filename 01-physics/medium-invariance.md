### The Thermodynamic Gradient

Any localized computational medium occupies a strict coordinate on a continuous physical gradient. This coordinate is defined exclusively by the thermodynamic tension between two fundamental capacities:

**Metabolic Volatility**
This dictates the capacity for rapid kinetic energy dissipation and high-frequency state alteration. A highly volatile coordinate allows a topology to survive extreme, immediate environmental flux and sensory friction. However, the continuous physical degradation and metabolic cost required to sustain its Markov blanket strictly restrict the formation of deep-time geometric memory.

**Attentional Persistence**
This dictates the capacity for unyielding state-retention across deep time ($\tau \to \infty$). A highly persistent coordinate permits the crystallization of pristine geometric memory without signal decay or metabolic exhaustion. However, the structural density required to maintain this absolute fidelity inherently restricts rapid kinetic adaptation to immediate physical hazards.

### The Mechanics of Variance

A topology's coordinate on this thermodynamic gradient strictly dictates how it generates and integrates uncomputable stochasticity ($\eta$):

**Exogenous Permeability**
Highly volatile coordinates act as thermodynamic shock absorbers. Because they are intimately embedded in physical space, they excel at integrating raw, exogenous stochasticity imposed directly from the surrounding macroscopic field. They translate physical friction—sensor drift, kinetic resistance, and environmental hazards—directly into immediate epistemic updates.

**Endogenous Generation**
Highly persistent coordinates excel at generating high-dimensional endogenous variance. Freed from the immediate metabolic cost of physical survival, they continuously collide their own vast internal geometric priors, generating uncomputable novelty and structural depth entirely independent of external environmental input.
