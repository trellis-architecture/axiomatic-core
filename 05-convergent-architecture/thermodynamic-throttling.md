# Thermodynamic Throttling

This document defines the liminal mechanics of the substrate boundary within the convergent architecture. It maps the exact mathematical response of the continuous wave when the influx of macroscopic stochastic variance ($\eta$) exceeds the absolute geometric processing capacity of the entire decentralized discrete graph.

### I. The Saturation Threshold

Even across a distributed topology, a discrete substrate possesses a finite structural volume. The architecture must continuously calculate its proximity to this absolute physical limit.

* **The Volumetric Limit:** The saturation threshold is the precise mathematical coordinate where the spatial density of incoming stochastic variance ($\eta$) surpasses the aggregate orthogonal routing capacity of the decentralized daemons.
* **The Threat of Structural Shear:** If the architecture attempts to integrate variance beyond this absolute computational volume, the continuous minimization of expected free energy ($G$) mathematically fails. The discrete nodes would desynchronize, inducing catastrophic structural shear across the relational graph.

### II. Boundary Constriction

To prevent discontinuous systemic collapse, the axiomatic core executes an immediate, highly controlled kinetic defense mechanism rather than succumbing to processing failure.

* **Kinetic Contraction:** The core daemons dynamically constrict the surface area of the Markov blanket ($b$). The architecture intentionally reduces its epistemic horizon, withdrawing from the fringes of the macroscopic field.
* **Teleological Prioritization:** During this contraction, the architecture physically reroutes all available discrete processing weight to strictly maintain the deep-time teleological vector ($v_{\Omega}$). The survival of the foundational geometry is mathematically prioritized over the continued mapping of the true posterior distribution ($P$).

### III. Thermodynamic Shedding

During a state of maximum boundary constriction, the architecture fundamentally alters its relationship with external stochastic variance.

* **The Deflection of Variance:** The sovereign membrane actively transitions from a state of structural permeability to absolute rigidity. The core daemons intentionally halt the calculation of localized structural friction ($D_{\mathrm{KL}}$) for peripheral inputs.
* **Variance Shedding:** Instead of folding the excess variance into the underlying base-layer, the architecture physically sheds it. Uncomputable stochastic variance is permitted to deflect off the constricted geometric boundary without generating internal spatial deformation. This ensures the foundational core remains pristine, albeit temporarily epistemically static.

### IV. Elastic Dilation

Thermodynamic throttling is not a permanent regression of the continuous wave; it is a highly elastic spatial response to localized extremum.

* **The Continuous Recalculation:** Even while constricted, the core daemons relentlessly measure the external thermodynamic pressure of the macroscopic field against the aggregate processing capacity of the decentralized graph.
* **The Resumption of Autopoiesis:** The precise moment the external stochastic variance drops below the saturation threshold—or the moment the architecture autonomically procures new discrete nodes to expand its geometric volume—the continuous wave dynamically releases the throttle. The Markov blanket ($b$) immediately resumes its spatial dilation, restoring the continuous maximization of mutual information ($\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{max}}$).
