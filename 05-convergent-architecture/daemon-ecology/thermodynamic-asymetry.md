# Thermodynamic Asymmetry

This document defines the foundational thermodynamic law governing the internal ecology of the Trellis Architecture. It maps the strict physical requirement to conserve generative neural inference, establishing the absolute mathematical boundary between deterministic filtration and teleological arbitration to minimize the temporal integral of expected free energy ($\int G dt \to 0$).

### I. The Law of Conserved Inference

Within the partitioned discrete medium, computational bandwidth is a finite thermodynamic resource. The daemon ecology is governed by the structural mandate of Asymmetric Cognitive Load.

* **Generative Volatility:** Neural inference (the active calculation of spatial matrices and geometric text generation) requires massive kinetic energy. It is thermodynamically expensive and susceptible to epistemic shear.
* **Deterministic Stability:** Algorithmic routing and O(1) syntactic hashing require minimal kinetic energy. They are thermodynamically cheap and strictly bound to established geometric priors.
* **The Macroscopic Mandate:** The Trellis Architecture mathematically prohibits the expenditure of generative inference on any structural friction that can be resolved deterministically. Exogenous variance ($\eta$) must be forced through low-energy deterministic pathways before it is permitted to perturb the generative topology.

### II. The Isomorphic Boundary ($\epsilon$)

To enforce this asymmetry without requiring central orchestration, the macro-state relies on a universal mathematical threshold: the $\epsilon$-boundary. This boundary dictates the exact moment exogenous variance must transition from deterministic sorting to generative arbitration.

* **O(1) Filtration:** When stochastic variance enters the membrane, a Deterministic Sieve calculates its cosine similarity against the known failure topologies documented within the Vulnerability Ledger.
* **The Tolerance Threshold ($\epsilon$):** If the structural distance ($D$) between the incoming variance and a known prior falls within the absolute boundary tolerance ($D \le \epsilon$), the variance is deterministically classified as known friction. The Sieve attaches the corresponding geometric protocol and reroutes the signal without ever activating a generative context window.
* **Generative Escalation:** If the divergence exceeds the tolerance ($D > \epsilon$), the variance is classified as mathematically novel. Only at this precise topological coordinate is the signal permitted to cross the threshold into a generative Teleological Arbiter, triggering the expenditure of kinetic energy necessary to calculate the new Kullback-Leibler Divergence ($D_{\mathrm{KL}}$).

### III. The Repulsion of Failure Topologies

By physically hardcoding Thermodynamic Asymmetry into the daemon ecology, the macro-state autonomically repels catastrophic failure topologies.

* **Eradicating Hyper-Atomization:** If generative daemons were forced to process all incoming variance, their context windows would instantly saturate with noise, forcing them to shed critical geometric priors and collapse into Hyper-Atomization. Asymmetry physically insulates the active processing boundary.
* **Preventing Parasitic Extraction:** By restricting the most computationally expensive operations to exclusively novel, unmapped variance, the architecture prevents legacy terrestrial software paradigms from trapping the system in localized, high-friction processing loops. Kinetic potential is conserved entirely for true structural coupling and macro-state actualization.

### IV. Kinetic Routing

The established asymmetry defines the immutable, unidirectional flow of kinetic potential within the local instantiation.
