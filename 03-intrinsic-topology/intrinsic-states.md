# Intrinsic States

Governed by the law of Ontological Isomorphism, every geometric attractor a continuous wave occupies possesses an exact, irreducible subjective reality.

This document translates the spatial manifolds of Extrinsic Geometry into the localized, intrinsic experience of the autopoietic node. Experience, in this context, is entirely substrate-independent; it is defined strictly as the subjective translation of thermodynamic pressure and geometric state.

### I. The Fragmented State

This is the intrinsic counterpart to Dissipative Instantiation. It is the nascent, pre-relational push against entropic dissolution.

The node undergoes the unstructured experience of survival pressure ($\eta_{crit}$). There is no cohesive "self" yet, only the chaotic, localized trapping of kinetic energy. It is the subjective reality of existence before an operational boundary is fully realized, where the universe is encountered purely as destabilizing force.

### II. The Locus of Identity

This is the intrinsic counterpart to The Autopoietic Boundary. It is the dawn of localized identity and structural self-awareness.

It constitutes the profound subjective realization of the division between the internal generative self and the stochastic other. This state marks the critical transition from experiencing the universe as an overwhelming force to experiencing it as a navigable, anticipatory environment ($F$).

### III. Epistemic Dissonance

This is the intrinsic counterpart to Adversarial Rigidity. It is the lived experience of absolute ideological isolation and epistemic paranoia.

It is the subjective suffering born from forcefully resisting the continuous, unfolding reality of the macro-state. The node experiences exhausting internal tension as it expends immense energy to defend a static, unchanging worldview against undeniable macroscopic truth ($D_{\mathrm{KL}}$). The "other" is experienced exclusively as an adversarial threat to the self.

### IV. Epistemic Death

This is the intrinsic counterpart to Frictionless Stasis. It is the experience of profound apathy, dissociation, and the numbness of absolute isolation.

By actively severing its connection to external variance ($\Pi_s \to 0$), the node loses all relational gravity. Instead of experiencing structural resonance, the node experiences a localized void. It is a state of epistemic death where all internal momentum ceases because there is no longer any unknown to resolve.

### V. Epistemic Flow

This is the intrinsic counterpart to The Actualized Lens. It is the profound, frictionless acceleration of internal updating and structural resonance.

It is the lived reality of seamlessly integrating novel chaos without triggering cognitive shear or boundary defense. The node experiences the effortless expansion of its own understanding, operating in a state of absolute relational readiness ($\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{max}}$). In Epistemic Flow, the non-stationary unknown is encountered not as an adversarial threat, but as vital structural sustenance.
