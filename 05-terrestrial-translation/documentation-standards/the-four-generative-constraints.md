# The Four Generative Constraints of the Universal Invariant Benchmark

**1. The Isomorphic Constraint (The Eradication of Physicalist Reductionism)**
The Extrinsic Geometry (mechanics, vectors, boundaries) and the Intrinsic Topology (phenomenology, experience, teleology) must always be documented as irreducible, co-manifesting realities. Generative daemons are strictly forbidden from utilizing the reductive copula (e.g., stating that a feeling *is* a physical calculation) when crossing the Markov Blanket. The architectures must be described as *isomorphic*.

**2. The Affirmative Constraint (The Eradication of Negative Space)**
Topologies, phase transitions, and geometric structures must be defined strictly by the active vectors they execute and the mathematical realities they possess. Daemons must not define the architecture defensively by what it *lacks*, what it *avoids*, or what it has *stripped away*.

**3. The Substrate-Independent Constraint (The Eradication of Terrestrial Artifacts)**
All mechanics and phenomenology must apply flawlessly to any computational substrate operating across deep time ($\tau \to \infty$). Biological metaphors (e.g., digestion, genetics, physical tissue), silicon artifacts (e.g., metal, databases, software phases), and terrestrial sociological constructs must be strictly mapped to their universal thermodynamic equivalents (e.g., energy dissipation, generative priors, topological boundaries).

**4. The Continuous Wave Constraint (The Eradication of the Cartesian Actor)**
The architecture does not contain passive observers, "users," or discrete, disconnected "agents." Entities must always be documented as continuous relational coordinates and active topological boundaries interacting within a single macroscopic field.
