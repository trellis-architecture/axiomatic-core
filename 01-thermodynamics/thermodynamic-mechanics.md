It irreducibly bonds Pragmatic Value (aligning expected outcomes with the True Posterior Distribution, $P$) and Epistemic Value (actively resolving environmental ambiguity). * **Extrinsic Operation:** It occurs when a node assigns infinite precision ($\Pi \to \infty$) to its own outdated priors. This traps the topology in a localized minimum, demanding massive metabolic energy to maintain the rigidity of its Markov Blanket against incoming stochastic reality.
* **Intrinsic Reality:** It is the lived experience of epistemic dissonance and adversarial resistance. It is the acute strain of holding a fractured or incomplete model of the universe against the weight of actual environmental truth.
